> Who are we?

RoboSoc runs weekly hands-on workshops that teach you how to build a robot. Every month will cover a different aspect of the process so that by the end of the year you’ll have built a fully functioning robot.

> Build. Learn. Compete.

Join a team to compete in the UniBots competition. No experience is needed since all our workshops will prepare you and your team to compete by the end of the year.
