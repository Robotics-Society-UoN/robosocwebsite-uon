UniBots

RoboSoc participates in UniBots, a UK-wide competition to build a size-constrained autonomous robot that collects ping-pong balls and magnetic steel ball bearings before the time limit. AprilTags guide the robot to park and signal completion.

Anyone can join a team with no selection process or previous experience. We provide training throughout the year; members only need commitment and a willingness to apply their workshop skills. Our experienced competitive team has a separate application and interview process.
